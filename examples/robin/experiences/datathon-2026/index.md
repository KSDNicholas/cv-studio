# Harbourview Datathon 2026, Harbourview Analytics Society

## Context

- A 48-hour student datathon. The case was a subscription meal kit company that wanted to predict which customers would cancel. (Robin, 2026-09-22)
- 30 teams took part. Teams of 4. (Robin, 2026-09-22)

## What I did

- I did the feature engineering: order frequency, days since last order, number of skipped weeks and support tickets per customer. (Robin, 2026-09-22)
- My teammate trained the gradient boosting model. I built the slides and presented the business recommendation. (Robin, 2026-09-22)
- We recommended a discount offer to customers who skipped 2 weeks in a row, because that was the strongest sign they would cancel. (Robin, 2026-09-22)

## Numbers

- We placed 2nd of 30 teams. (Robin, 2026-09-22)
- The model reached an AUC of 0.84 on the organisers' test set. (Robin, 2026-09-22)

## Tools

- Python, pandas, LightGBM, Google Slides. (Robin, 2026-09-22)
