# BrightMart: Data Analyst Intern

Source: pasted in CV Studio, 2026-10-01.

BrightMart is a fictional online grocery retailer used as an example in cv-studio.

Data Analyst Intern (6 months, from January 2027)

About the team
Our Commercial Analytics team helps buyers, marketing and operations make decisions with data. We look after the dashboards the business runs on every day and answer the questions that come up in between.

What you will do
- Write SQL to pull and clean data from our warehouse
- Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau or Power BI for buyers and operations managers
- Analyse delivery performance, basket size and customer retention, and present findings to stakeholders
- Help design and read A/B tests on the website
- Document datasets so other analysts can reuse them

What we are looking for
- Currently studying for a degree in analytics, statistics, computer science, economics or similar
- Strong SQL
- Experience with a BI tool (Tableau or Power BI)
- Python or R for analysis
- Clear communication: you can explain a number to someone who does not work with data

Nice to have
- Experience with dbt or data modelling
- Knowledge of A/B testing and basic statistics
- Interest in retail or e-commerce

Please state your availability.
